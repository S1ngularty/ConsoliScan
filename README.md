# ConsoliScan

ConsoliScan is a modern retail management ecosystem designed for Philippine retailers. It combines a Point-of-Sale (POS) system, inventory management, AI-assisted product scanning, and BNPC government subsidy verification into a unified web and mobile platform. Built with an offline-first architecture, the system enables businesses to continue operating even in areas with unreliable internet connectivity.

## Features

* Point-of-Sale (POS) transaction management
* AI-assisted barcode and product recognition
* Real-time inventory and product management
* Offline-first transactions with automatic synchronization
* BNPC government subsidy eligibility verification
* Multi-role access (Customer, Checker, Merchandiser, Admin, Super Admin)
* Sales analytics and reporting
* Loyalty rewards system
* Blockchain-backed transaction audit trail

## Why ConsoliScan?

ConsoliScan is designed for businesses of all sizes, from small neighborhood stores to large retail establishments. Through its mobile applications, retailers can manage products, inventory, and transactions directly from a smartphone, eliminating the need for dedicated POS hardware. This makes digital retail management more accessible and affordable for small and medium-sized enterprises while providing the scalability required for larger businesses.

By integrating AI, offline capabilities, secure authentication, and government subsidy verification into a single platform, ConsoliScan delivers a reliable, efficient, and future-ready retail solution.
